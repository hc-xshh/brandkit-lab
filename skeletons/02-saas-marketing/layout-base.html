Questions

Answered before you have to ask

Does Ledgerline move money?

No. It holds read-only bank credentials and never initiates a payment. Writes happen only in your billing system, and only when you approve a match.

What happens when a rule is wrong?

You edit the rule, and the change is versioned. Every invoice it touched is listed so you can re-run just those.

Where is the data stored?

In Frankfurt, encrypted at rest, with a documented retention window you can shorten or extend.

Can I export everything and leave?

Yes: matches, rules and decisions export as CSV and JSON from the settings page, without asking us.
