The contract

What you are buying, and what you are not

This is a protection product. It pays a lump sum if the life insured dies within the selected term; it builds no cash value, and there is nothing to surrender if you stop paying. Premiums are level for the term and are not reviewed at each anniversary.

Underwriting happens after the application, and the price you see here assumes no medical evidence has been requested yet. If the insurer asks for evidence and the terms change, you may accept the new terms or withdraw within the cooling-off period with a full refund.

Claims are paid to the beneficiaries you nominate. If no nomination is in force, the benefit forms part of the estate and may be subject to inheritance tax, which is why we ask for a nomination at application and remind you to review it.

Questions we are asked most

Plain answers to the awkward questions

What is excluded from cover?

Suicide within the first twenty-four months, and claims arising from facts that were not disclosed at application. The full list is in the conditions, not in a footnote.

Can the premium change during the term?

No. The premium is fixed when the contract starts and applies for the whole term, provided premiums are paid when due.

What happens if I stop paying?

Cover ends. There is no surrender value to collect because the contract holds no investment account.

How long does a claim take?

Ten working days from a complete file. If a document is missing, we say which one in a single request rather than a series of them.
