What is covered

Cover that pays out once, and pays in full

The policy pays the sum insured if the life insured dies within the selected term. There is no investment account attached to this contract and therefore no account value to surrender.

Death benefit

Paid to the nominated beneficiaries within ten working days of a complete claim file. The sum insured is fixed at the start of the contract and is not reduced by the insurer's own costs.

Terminal illness advance

Where a treating physician certifies a life expectancy of twelve months or less, the benefit may be advanced before death, at no additional premium.

What is not covered

We would rather lose the sale than surprise a family at the worst possible moment.

Claims arising in the first twenty-four months where the application omitted a material fact are limited to a refund of premiums. Suicide within twenty-four months is excluded, as required by the jurisdiction in which the policy is issued.

Full exclusions are set out in the policy conditions and the key information document.

Premiums and taxes

What you pay, and what we are obliged to withhold

Premiums are level for the term of the contract. Premium tax, where applicable, is included in the quoted figure. We do not apply a policy fee, an issue fee or a broker charge beyond the disclosed commission.

If you change your mind

You have thirty days from the start date to cancel and receive a full refund of premiums paid, provided no claim has been made. After that, the policy may be cancelled at any time and cover ends when premiums stop.

Cooling-off period: 30 days, full refund.

How we handle your data

Personal data is processed under the general data protection regulation, stored inside the European economic area, and retained for the statutory period after the contract ends. Medical evidence is used only for underwriting and claim assessment.
